# 1) Hardware BOM

## Battery Core
- Battery type: Lithium-Ion pack (commonly LiFePO4 or NMC), usually 13S/14S architecture
- Nominal voltage: 48V–51.2V
- Housing: Long ribbed aluminum case with top handle + key ignition
- Charging input: 3-pin XLR

## Phase 1: Structure & Enclosure
- Aluminum extrusion instrument box (depth for battery + electronics allowance)
- Internal mounting plate: 1/16" or 1/8" aluminum sheet
- Heavy-duty external carry handle
- Rubber stabilization feet
- Nylon PCB standoffs
- Rubber wiring grommets

## Phase 2: Safety & Distribution
- 30A ANL fuse + insulated holder
- Marine-grade master disconnect rotary switch
- Dual insulated copper bus bars (positive/negative)
- 8 AWG OFC wire for 48V trunk

## Phase 3: Conversion Modules
- 48V to 120V pure sine inverter (1000W–1200W continuous)
- 48V to 12V DC-DC converter (20A–30A continuous)
- 48V battery monitor LCD with inline low-side shunt
- 3x 12V LED toggle switches (AC/DC/USB zones)

## Output Interface Targets
- AC: 1–2 x 120V outlets
- USB: 2 x USB-C PD (up to 100W) + 2 x USB-A QC 3.0
- DC high current: XT60 + Anderson Powerpole
- DC low current: 12V cigarette socket + 5.5 x 2.1mm barrel
